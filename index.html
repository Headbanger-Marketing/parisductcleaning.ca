FAQs

Questions Paris Homeowners Ask

Pricing, certification, timing, and the air itself, covered below.

How often should a Paris home have its ducts cleaned?

Two to three years is the honest range. Valley homes that run dehumidifiers hard, houses with hearth habits or pets, and anything recently renovated should lean toward the two-year end. When in doubt, we will scope a trunk line and let the footage decide.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified, trained, and fully insured, every job, documents shown on request. Ontario does not run a trade licence for duct cleaning, which makes certification and insurance the checks worth asking about, and we volunteer both.

How do I know the visit accomplished anything?

Camera footage from your own trunk lines, captured before and after and reviewed together before we leave. In a town this particular about its buildings, that footage travels by word of mouth, and we are fine with that.

Our dryer struggles more in winter than summer. Vent?

That pattern points at the vent, a cold, humid termination icing or packing at the bends. We clear from both ends and meter the draft, and the seasonal struggle usually disappears with the lint.

What does a visit involve, and how long?

Suction set at the furnace, registers washed, each run worked from its opening, blower compartment cleared, footage captured. Hillside and multi-storey Paris homes run four to five hours, single-storey east-side builds less.

The house holds a smell we cannot place. Ducts?

Often. Old stone holds odour in many places, but the duct walls recirculate theirs every cycle. Cleaning lifts the carrier, and a sanitizer treatment on the exposed metal finishes what remains.

Why is one room always dusty and the rest fine?

That room usually sits at the end of the longest or leakiest run. Sealing the joints along the way restores its share of airflow and stops the dust settling that uneven systems create, and we can map exactly that.

Do you work beyond Paris?

Yes, across Brant County and the approaches to Waterloo, Brantford, St. George, Ayr, Glen Morris, Cambridge, and Princeton are all on the regular schedule.

Do you serve areas outside of Paris, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Paris and nearby communities including Brantford, St. George, Ayr, Glen Morris, and the surrounding area.
